苏州，一园一景Suzhou, garden by garden

Suzhou grew up on canals and white-walled courtyards. This guide follows eleven places in a loose route: the old lanes and gardens on the east side, the south gate, the gardens and temples in the west, and a modern finish by the lake. Scroll the stories, or select a number on the map. 苏州因运河和粉墙黛瓦的院落而兴。本指南沿一条松散的路线串起十一处地点：东侧的老街与园林、南面的城门、西边的园林与古寺，最后在湖边看看现代苏州。向下滚动阅读，或点击地图上的数字。
